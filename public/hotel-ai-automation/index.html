Digital worker approach

OpenClaw is not another dashboard for the team to manage.

It is a controlled digital worker layer for hotel work that repeats every day: guest questions, booking follow-ups, lead capture, internal routing, and owner summaries.

Each worker listens to one workflow, uses hotel-approved knowledge, takes the next practical action, and hands off to a person when judgement is needed. The hotel stays in control. The repetitive work stops sitting inside WhatsApp chats, memory, and scattered reminders.
