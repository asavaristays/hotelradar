# HotelRADAR Chain Presentation Script

Last updated: March 2026

## Opening

HotelRADAR is a production revenue intelligence system for hotels operating in Goa and Mumbai.

It helps revenue managers and hotel owners understand:
- where demand is strengthening
- where current pricing is below market
- where OTA leakage exists
- which stay dates justify immediate pricing action

This is not a PMS and not an auto-publishing engine.

It is a pricing decision cockpit.

## Problem Statement

Many hotels still make pricing decisions through:
- manual OTA checks
- fragmented competitor tracking
- reactive event awareness
- individual judgment without structured demand visibility

That causes:
- missed surge dates
- underpricing
- parity leakage
- inconsistent pricing discipline

## What HotelRADAR Does

HotelRADAR combines:
- competitor movement
- OTA parity
- city event signals
- seasonality
- holiday effects
- airfare and market context

and turns that into:
- a demand score
- a suggested price
- a clear market position
- a date-by-date forward demand view
- a narrative explaining why action is recommended

## What Makes It Useful

The product is designed to answer practical revenue questions:
- should we hold, raise, or review rate for this date?
- are we below market?
- are OTA channels leaking revenue?
- are upcoming events likely to change demand?

## Current Operating Scope

Today the live operating markets are:
- Goa
- Mumbai

This narrow scope is deliberate.

It allows stronger market configuration, cleaner competitor mapping, and better signal quality.

## Likely Questions

### Is this fully automated?

No. It is advisory. The hotel remains in control of final pricing action.

### Do you support all cities?

No. We currently operate in Goa and Mumbai to maintain quality and reliability.

### Can this replace a full RMS?

Not the positioning today. The value today is actionable revenue intelligence and pricing guidance in active markets.

## Close

HotelRADAR gives hotel teams a clearer answer to one core question:

> For this stay date, are we priced correctly relative to market demand and market movement?

That is the immediate value.

In Goa and Mumbai, that value is already operationally meaningful.
