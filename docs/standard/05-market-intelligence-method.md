# How HotelRADAR creates market intelligence

## Product thesis

Market Intelligence is created by connecting external market data, hotel-specific data, and commercial context into a daily decision story.

The system should not only say “increase” or “reduce.” It should say:

- what changed;
- why the date matters;
- what the hotel is doing right or wrong;
- what evidence is missing;
- what revenue action is safe;
- what sales opportunity can be pursued;
- what digital gap may be hurting conversion.

## Intelligence layers

### Layer 1: Evidence capture

Collect observations:

- official rate;
- OTA rate;
- competitor rate;
- event/holiday;
- MICE;
- wedding;
- travel/search;
- weather/risk;
- digital asset signals;
- PMS pickup once integrated.

### Layer 2: Verification

Each observation is checked for:

- source;
- timestamp;
- stay date;
- freshness;
- normalization;
- confidence;
- duplicate risk;
- contradiction risk.

### Layer 3: Normalization

Rates must be normalized before comparison:

- currency;
- room category;
- occupancy count;
- tax inclusion;
- meal plan;
- cancellation policy;
- stay date;
- source freshness.

### Layer 4: Signal classification

Classify signals into groups:

- price evidence;
- demand pressure;
- commercial opportunity;
- digital asset intelligence;
- risk;
- data health.

### Layer 5: Decision contract

Central Intelligence decides:

- action;
- confidence;
- lock status;
- missing requirements;
- top drivers;
- contradiction notes.

### Layer 6: Client story

The client-facing output explains:

- market read;
- where the hotel may be going wrong;
- commercial actions;
- signal readiness;
- digital asset watch;
- opportunity view;
- missing evidence.

## Where the hotel may be going wrong

Examples:

- priced above market without pickup validation;
- priced below market during demand compression;
- OTA parity or public rate leakage;
- no direct booking clarity;
- weak Google Business Profile or review momentum;
- MICE/wedding signals not captured;
- discounts open on high-pressure dates;
- stale competitor evidence.

## Why this can justify paid value

At Rs. 280 per room per month, the product must save or create more value than its cost by:

- preventing underpricing during demand compression;
- stopping unnecessary discounting;
- identifying group/MICE/wedding opportunity;
- reducing OTA leakage;
- improving direct-channel awareness;
- warning owners before data goes stale;
- giving the team a daily action list.

## Important limitation

The product should clearly separate:

- verified live data;
- structured pilot data;
- manual verified data;
- estimated/supporting data;
- missing data.

Client trust depends on this separation.
