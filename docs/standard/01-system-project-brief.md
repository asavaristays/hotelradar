# System and project brief

## Objective

HotelRADAR is a subscription-based Hotel Market Intelligence product for Indian hotels.

Its aim is to give each hotel a daily, decision-ready view of:

- market pricing movement;
- OTA and public-channel evidence;
- competitor rate pressure;
- holidays, events, MICE, wedding, and weekend demand pressure;
- travel/search/airfare movement;
- weather or disruption risk;
- digital asset gaps;
- revenue and sales opportunities;
- safe pricing or inventory actions.

The commercial ambition is to make the product worth a room-based monthly fee, for example Rs. 280 per room per month, by giving a hotel owner or GM sharper market visibility than a traditional RMS alone.

## What makes it different from traditional RMS

Traditional RMS often focuses on internal pickup, occupancy, historical demand, and rule-based rate suggestions. HotelRADAR should move beyond that by adding:

- external market signals;
- current public price evidence;
- competitor and comp-set movement;
- market demand context;
- opportunity classification for revenue and sales teams;
- digital asset weakness detection;
- transparent evidence readiness;
- daily narrative explaining why a recommendation is safe or blocked.

## Current product output

Daily Market Intelligence email and PDF should include:

- greeting and context;
- property and stay date;
- recommended action;
- confidence/trust status;
- market read;
- where the hotel may be going wrong;
- commercial actions;
- digital asset watch;
- signal readiness table;
- opportunity view;
- missing evidence / next actions.

## Success criteria

The product is strong when a busy GM can understand in under two minutes:

- what matters today;
- why it matters;
- what risk exists;
- what revenue action is safe;
- what sales opportunity should be pursued;
- what data is still missing.
