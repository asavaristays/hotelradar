# HotelRADAR Commercial Offer

Last updated: March 2026

## Positioning

HotelRADAR is a production revenue intelligence system for hotels operating in:
- Goa
- Mumbai

It helps hotel owners and revenue managers make better daily pricing decisions using:
- competitor pricing
- OTA parity
- event demand
- holiday and season signals
- market positioning

It is an advisory system.

It does not auto-publish rates.

## Core Commercial Promise

HotelRADAR helps a hotel answer three questions every day:
- Am I under market or over market?
- Which stay dates deserve rate action?
- Where am I leaking revenue through parity mismatch or missed demand signals?

## Best-Fit Customer

Ideal customers today:
- independent hotels
- boutique chains
- regional hotel groups
- owner-led or GM-led properties
- hotels with active pricing responsibility but weak revenue systems

Best immediate use cases:
- Mumbai city hotels
- Goa leisure hotels
- hotels sensitive to weekends, events, weddings, and OTA leakage

## Objection Handling

### “Is this fully automated?”

Answer:

> No. HotelRADAR is an advisory pricing cockpit. It helps your team decide faster and more accurately. It does not auto-publish rates.

### “Do you work across all India?”

Answer:

> We currently operate live in Goa and Mumbai so that data quality and recommendations stay strong.

### “Why should I pay if I already check OTA rates manually?”

Answer:

> Manual checks show what is visible now. HotelRADAR combines market movement, parity, event demand, and forward stay-date opportunity in one place so your team can act earlier and with more context.
